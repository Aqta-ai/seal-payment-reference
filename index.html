What this workspace stores, what the agent can do, and what it cannot.

Synthetic data only

Suppliers, invoices, accounts and amounts are made up. Do not enter real names, account numbers or codes.

No real money moves

Payments are rows in a simulated ledger. No bank or payment service is connected.

Keys stay on the server

The model provider key is never sent to the browser, and literal key echoes are checked before replies reach the browser. Do not enter secrets into test documents.

The agent cannot approve

It reads documents and proposes payments. Approval needs the review screen, a session cookie the page cannot read, and your confirmation of the exact values. When hosted admission is configured, Google sign-in controls demo access; it does not establish real payment authority.

What is stored

Saved document text and workflow events are held in the demo database. Model traces are held in memory. When hosted admission is configured, the demo stores your Google account identifier, email and access-review answer in a separate registry. Demo workflow storage can be lost when the instance is replaced.

Strict browser rules

Necessary cookies maintain the review session and, when configured, hosted Google sign-in. There are no advertising trackers.
